# Game Design Document

## Core Concept
SNIPER ELITE is a tactical sniper-action game where the player infiltrates enemy zones, secures objectives, and eliminates high-value targets under pressure.

## Objective
Complete chapter-based missions with stealth, accuracy, and tactical timing.

## Core Gameplay Loops
- Select mission
- Navigate environment
- Track enemy positions
- Eliminate targets
- Extract or complete objective
- Earn rewards and unlock upgrades

## Player Experience
- Tactical pacing
- Precision shooting
- Rewarding progression
- Strong sense of mission completion

## Progression System
- weapon unlocks
- attachments
- mission rewards
- chapter unlocks
- score and star rating
